How it works

  1. 1

    Post or search

    Choose home to campus or campus to home, then enter your area, days and time. It takes under a minute.

  2. 2

    Send a request

    Pick a ride that suits you. The driver accepts or declines, and you get an email either way.

  3. 3

    Travel and rate

    Meet at a public pickup point, share the fuel cost, then rate the trip to help others.

Built for trust and safety

  • ✔ Verified student

    Everyone signs up with a university email address.

  • Private by default

    Only first names and public pickup points are shown. Home addresses stay hidden.

  • Ratings you can see

    Check a driver's or rider's rating before you send a request. Report or block anyone, any time.
